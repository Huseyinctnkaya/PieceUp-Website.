Features
Features

Every detail is yours to set.

Image, copy, piece count, reward and frequency — configured in one screen in your Shopify admin, live in your store the moment you save.

Build the puzzle

Any image becomes a game in a minute.

Upload a product shot, a lookbook photo, or a seasonal graphic. Set the pieces, the difficulty, and — if you want the clock adding pressure — a time limit.

  • 2×2 up to 4×4 pieces, three difficulty levels
  • Optional time limit for extra urgency
  • Your own headline, description, and accent color
  • Preview it exactly as a shopper would, before you publish
Rewards

Real Shopify discount codes, minted on the win.

The puzzle opens right inside your storefront. Finish the picture and the reward appears — no email gate, no redirect, no manual code to send.

  • Percentage off, amount off, or a free "try again"
  • Limit any prize to specific products or collections
  • Codes are single-use and show up under Discounts like any other
  • Works on mobile and desktop
Triggers & frequency

Show it where — and how often — makes sense.

Turn the widget on from your theme editor, then decide exactly when it should get a shopper's attention.

  • A button, an auto-popup after a delay, or both
  • Every page, product pages only, or the cart
  • Play limits: once ever, once a day, or unlimited — enforced per shopper
  • A guided setup checklist walks you through going live
Analytics

Know exactly what the game earns.

Revenue attributed to PieceUp codes, not just clicks and impressions — with cancellations deducted so the number stays honest.

  • Opens, completions, rewards given, and codes redeemed
  • Revenue earned and average order value from those orders
  • A daily breakdown, and an alert when shoppers finish but a monthly reward limit blocks their code
  • No personal shopper data stored — just what happened and what it was worth
A/B testing · Premium

Run two, keep the winner.

Each shopper always sees the same variant, so the comparison stays clean. Change one thing — the image, the reward, the piece count — and let the revenue decide.

  • Even split by default, or set your own percentage
  • Sticky assignment — no shopper flips between variants mid-test
  • One click to promote the winner once you're confident
  • Reserved for Premium — a real test needs the traffic that tier's stores have
