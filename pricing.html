Pricing FAQ

What counts as a "reward"?

Any prize handed out when a shopper finishes a puzzle — a discount code or a free "try again." Your monthly limit resets on your billing date.

What happens if I hit my monthly limit?

Shoppers can still finish the puzzle, but PieceUp can't mint a code past your plan's limit — your analytics will flag exactly how many finishers that affected, so you know if it's time to upgrade.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Downgrading is prorated — you're credited for the unused days on your current plan, right from your Shopify billing.

Why is A/B testing Premium-only?

A test needs real traffic — usually at least a hundred visitors per variant — before it can confidently name a winner. Stores with that much traffic tend to be past Pro's monthly reward allowance anyway, so the gate lands where the feature is actually usable.

Still deciding?

The free plan has no time limit — build a puzzle and see how it feels in your store first.
