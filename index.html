What's inside

Everything you need to run it, nothing you have to babysit

Build from any image

2×2 up to 4×4 pieces, three difficulty levels, an optional time limit.

Rewards you control

Percentage off, amount off, or a no-cost "try again" — limited to products or collections if needed.
